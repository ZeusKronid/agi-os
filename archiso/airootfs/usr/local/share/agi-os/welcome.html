AGI OS Installer

Open AGI OS Installer from the applications menu, or run agi-installer. Choose your LLM provider, connect, and describe the system you want.

Choose from suggestions, ask for a recommendation, or propose your own desktop, window manager, applications and settings.

  1. Start the live system, connect to the network and your provider.
  2. Describe the system you want.
  3. Choose the destination disk and discuss the storage layout.
  4. Review the complete configuration and confirm the exact disk changes.
  5. The installer writes the system and selected packages.
  6. The installer configures boot, access and your environment.
  7. Shut down, disconnect the ISO and boot from the installed disk.
  8. Run agi-os-verify --gui or agi-os-verify, test your applications, and reboot again to verify that your data and settings persist.

Enter provider keys and account passwords only in the application's private fields. Provider credentials are not copied to the new system.
